# Google Stitch Export Review

## Export received

The supplied Stitch ZIP contains customer-storefront concepts for:

- Home — desktop and mobile;
- Browse / Mhenching Finds — desktop and mobile;
- Product Detail — desktop and mobile;
- Checkout — desktop and mobile;
- Christmas Home — multiple mobile variants;
- Christmas Browse — mobile;
- Christmas Product Detail — mobile;
- shader/visual experiments;
- `Quiet Tropical Morning` design system.

## Approved as direction

- warm ivory surfaces;
- forest green primary actions;
- sage secondary tones;
- restrained terracotta accent;
- generous whitespace;
- tactile but restrained cards;
- Plus Jakarta Sans headings;
- Be Vietnam Pro body copy;
- “Quiet Commerce” philosophy;
- calm local trust cues;
- mobile-first discovery.

## Not approved as production truth

Stitch-generated product names, prices, stock quantities, delivery promises, maker identities, product imagery/provenance, checkout/payment rules, and generic foreign sample content are placeholders until backed by real Mhenching data and the PRD.

## Required refinement

1. Convert money examples to Philippine pesos.
2. Make Sta. Magdalena fulfillment explicit but calm.
3. Add Gawang Magdalena as a first-class lane.
4. Keep Christmas a seasonal skin, not the permanent identity.
5. Make stock messaging driven by bridge state.
6. Preserve accessibility contrast even with the soft palette.
7. Avoid luxury cues that imply high prices.
8. Avoid heavy animation that slows low-end mobile devices.
9. Design graceful poor-connectivity/loading/offline states.
10. Design unavailable/reservation-failed states before production.

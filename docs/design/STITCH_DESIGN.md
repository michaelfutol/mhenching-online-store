# Brand & Style

The design system is built on **Quiet Commerce** — a rejection of high-pressure retail tactics in favor of a serene, community-focused shopping experience. It should evoke the feeling of a breeze moving through a well-organized neighborhood store.

The style blends **Soft Minimalism** and **Tactile Modernism**. It prioritizes whitespace, organic color transitions, subtle depth and grounded interactions.

## Colors

- Main canvas: warm ivory / rice-paper tones.
- Surfaces/cards: white and soft neutral layers.
- Primary actions: deep forest green.
- Secondary actions/decor: muted sage.
- Accent: restrained terracotta; mango only for friendly/seasonal highlights.
- Text: natural charcoal, never harsh pure black unless accessibility requires.

## Typography

- **Plus Jakarta Sans** for headings.
- **Be Vietnam Pro** for body copy and labels.
- Generous line heights; readable first, decorative second.

## Layout & spacing

- 8px rhythmic scale.
- Desktop: 12-column grid, 1200px max width, 24px gutters.
- Mobile: 4-column grid, 16px side margins.
- Avoid information density; product grids should feel abundant without clutter.

## Elevation & depth

Use tonal layering and soft ambient shadows rather than heavy drop shadows. Hover/focus states should feel gently lifted, not aggressively recolored.

## Shapes

- buttons/inputs/small cards: ~8px radius;
- large cards/hero surfaces: ~16px radius;
- chips/badges may use pill geometry.

## Components

- Primary buttons: forest green, white text, tactile press state.
- Inputs: subtle sage border; forest-green focus state.
- Product cards: white, soft shadow, image-first, breathable.
- Tags: low-contrast sage-tinted backgrounds, no hard borders.
- Breadcrumb concept: “The Porch” — quiet path indicator, never dominant.

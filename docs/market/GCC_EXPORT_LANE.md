# GCC Export Lane — Deferred Research Track

## Intent

Treat GCC markets as a future growth lane for **Gawang Magdalena / Made in Sorsogon** products after the Mhenching Online MVP proves domestic demand and operational reliability.

Priority categories:

- finished native handicrafts and giftable decor;
- pili confectionery and shelf-stable gift products;
- food-grade and cosmetic-grade pili oil as separate regulated products;
- premium gift boxes aimed at Filipino diaspora and GCC gifting occasions.

## Initial market hypothesis

Start with diaspora and gifting demand, then validate broader local GCC interest. Avoid assuming one GCC-wide customer profile; research UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Bahrain, Kuwait and Oman separately when this track is activated.

## Entry principles

1. Prove product quality, packaging and fulfillment domestically first.
2. Use real Magdalena/Sorsogon photography and maker provenance.
3. Build export-readiness status per SKU before enabling international checkout.
4. Validate destination-country food/cosmetic/import rules product by product.
5. Prefer small, high-value, low-breakage, giftable goods before bulky commodity products.
6. Test demand with landing pages, diaspora communities, B2B buyers and paid social before holding export-specific stock.

## Hold point

This track is intentionally deferred while app development and the Christmas 2026 domestic launch remain the critical path.
